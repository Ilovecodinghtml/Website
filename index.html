First off, what is the "Plastisphere"?

The Plastisphere is a term used to describe the state of the Earth as a place filled with plastic. The term was coined in 2013 by marine biologist Linda Amaral-Zetter. This name was chosen for many different reasons, like microbial biofilms in water sources, the constant pollution of oceans, and the plastic material that settles in the soil/ground.

What is the GPGP?

The GPGP or Great Pacific Garbage Patch is an area that is filled with micro-plastics and trash but most of it isn't even visible and isn't an island but instead a loose clump of trash that all together takes up about the same area as Texas! Also it is very hard to clean it up so don't really bother but we can stop polluting the ocean and let the plastics dissolve then with better technology we can clean it up instead of letting it getting worse and making harder for our descendants do clean it.
